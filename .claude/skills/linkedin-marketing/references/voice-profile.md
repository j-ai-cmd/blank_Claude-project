## 1. Voice fingerprint

- Sentence rhythm: short and direct; one longer observational sentence every few lines; no fluff
- Signature openers: first-person observation or a specific number; never a question
- Punctuation habits: uses `..` as a soft pause naturally; minimal em dashes
- Words and phrases you use a lot: "built for their firm", "custom", "hours per week", "conversations", "gap"
- Words and phrases you NEVER use: leverage, streamline, harness, game-changer, unlock, fast-paced world, deep dive
- Emoji: none
- Formatting: short paragraphs, blank lines between ideas, occasional bold headers in lists
