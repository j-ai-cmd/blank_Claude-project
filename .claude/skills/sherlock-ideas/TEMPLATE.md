# Pitch file template

Save as `~/JAI_INSTA/shows/sherlock/ideas/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`. Order the ideas by score, highest first.

```md
# Sherlock ideas: <YYYY-MM-DD>

| # | Title | Format | Hook | Debate | Fresh | Buildable | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ... | what-if / under-the-hood / sitcom | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 18 |

## 1. <Title as on-screen hook>

- **Format:** A what-if / B under-the-hood / C sitcom
- **The mystery (first 2s):** "Observe. ..." (Holmes' voice)
- **Why people will argue:** side 1 vs side 2
- **Beat sketch:** 5–8 one-line beats (what-if: the time jumps; teardown: the layers; sitcom: setup, then punchline)
- **Verdict line:** the one-line takeaway before "Elementary, isn't it?"
- **Comment trigger:** the question, or "Comment WORD for ..."
- **Kit visuals:** which components (timeline, counter, chat bubbles, diagram)
- **Assets Jai would supply:** logos/screenshots, or "none"
- **Length:** 20–30s (sitcom) / 45–60s
- **Sources:** URLs for every fact
- **Risk check:** anything to soften or verify before scripting
```
